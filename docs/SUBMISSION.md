**Project name:** LiqShield — Collateral & Liquidity Stress Intelligence Core

**Selected track:** B. LiqShield (Money, risk and financial derivatives)

**Problem statement (short):**
Can a financial institution meet time-sensitive obligations (margin calls, settlements, fees) with the cash and
collateral it can actually mobilise? Headline collateral overstates liquidity: assets are encumbered, haircut, and
take time to monetise, while inflows can be delayed. LiqShield computes eligible collateral, simulates margin calls and
settlement delays across an 8-day time ladder, compares funding actions subject to lead times and limits, and reports
shortfall severity, time to shortfall and intervention cost.

**How Exasol is used:**
Every calculation runs in Exasol SQL joins, eligibility filters, time-bucket aggregation, window-function
cumulative ladders, a funding waterfall using window sums — plus an in-database Python UDF for Monte Carlo haircut
shocks. Each dashboard slider issues a parameterised query to Exasol Personal.

**Working solution:** FastAPI + Streamlit on top of Exasol Personal (see README §3A).
